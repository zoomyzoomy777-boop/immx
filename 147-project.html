147 Project is a dark-fantasy game prototype. The player follows The Kid through a haunted forest, meets its spirits and fights the creatures that live there.

We built the forest scenes, spirit magic and combat in Unreal Engine, and prototyped a dress-up system and crowd test in Unity. The clips on this page are work-in-progress captures from development.
